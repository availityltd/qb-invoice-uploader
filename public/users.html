Users

Anyone who signs in with their own QuickBooks address lands here as Reports only: they read and download everything and change nothing, until you say otherwise. Their QuickBooks key is never kept, so signing in can neither take the company over nor stop the app for everyone else.

A company admin is named on the server, not on this page. Courier accounts and their keys stay with the admin whatever else is given away.

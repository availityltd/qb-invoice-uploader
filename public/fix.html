This page reads your invoices so you can review them. Nothing is changed until you press a button on a row.

Take a backup first. Deleting an invoice in QuickBooks cannot be undone. Go to QuickBooks → Settings → Export data before using the delete buttons on this page. Try one row, check the result in QuickBooks, and only then carry on.

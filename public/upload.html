It keeps going on its own: the hosting connection is cut after about fifteen minutes. When that happens the app reconnects by itself within a few seconds and carries on from the exact invoice where it stopped. Nothing is skipped and nothing is uploaded twice.

Failures are retried: anything that fails is tried again automatically, up to three more rounds, with a growing pause between them.

Safe to close: progress is saved as it goes. If you close the app the button becomes Resume and picks up where it stopped.

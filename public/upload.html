What happens: invoices are created one at a time with a pause between each, so QuickBooks does not lock the inventory items. A failed invoice is retried up to five times before it is recorded as failed.

Safe to re-run: only the failed invoices are re-sent when you press Retry — the ones that succeeded are not duplicated.

It runs on the server: once you press Start the work carries on by itself. You can open another page, or close this one, and come back — the log and the progress will still be here.

Pause holds your place. Press Pause to hold the run where it is, then Carry on when you are ready — nothing already in QuickBooks is sent a second time. Stop ends the run, and Carry on still works afterwards.

Failures are retried: anything that fails is tried again automatically, up to four more rounds, with a growing pause between them.

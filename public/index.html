Send to Upload passes the converted invoices straight to the Upload page — no file needed. Download gives you the CSV files for a manual QuickBooks import instead.

Matching: each Shopify line SKU is looked up in your QuickBooks product list, and the matching Product/Service name is written to the output. Lines whose SKU is not in the list keep the Shopify name, and QuickBooks will reject them — add those products first, export the list again, and re-run.

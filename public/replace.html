For every variant live in the store, the item carrying its SKU is kept, and its duplicates in QuickBooks — the same product, the same shade — are paired onto it. A SKU that matches no item, or more than one, is set aside and listed, never guessed at. Nothing changes until the pairs have been read and Replace them is pressed.

Many products at once — paste the pairs

Two columns copied out of Excel — the item going on the left, the item taking its place on the right — one pair to a line. Each side is matched whole, on the item's name or its SKU, never on part of a name. A name that matches two items is not guessed at: it is sent back to be written out in full.

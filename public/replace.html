01 Which product becomes which

Two columns copied out of Excel — the item going on the left, the item taking its place on the right — one pair to a line. Each side is matched whole, on the item's name or its SKU, never on part of a name. A name that matches two items is not guessed at: it is sent back to be written out in full.

03 Where to look

The bills, expenses and credits are a short read. Invoices here run to tens of thousands a month and are read a day at a time, so a long range of them takes a while — the page can be closed, and the read carries on. Journal entries and deposits carry no product, only an account; change those on the Category tab.
